description: "CrimeWatch by BetterGov.PH is an independent, nonpartisan, open-source civic technology platform that makes Philippine crime and public safety statistics more accessible, understandable, and transparent. It transforms publicly available data from government agencies and reputable organizations into interactive maps, analytical dashboards, and meaningful visualizations, enabling users to explore crime trends, compare regional statistics, and better understand the country's peace and order situation. The platform helps citizens make informed decisions, researchers and data scientists conduct evidence-based studies, journalists produce data-driven reports, policymakers identify public safety challenges, and civil society organizations advocate for transparency and accountability. By promoting open data, responsible data interpretation, and community-driven collaboration, CrimeWatch aims to strengthen public awareness, encourage informed civic participation, and support evidence-based governance across the Philippines."
