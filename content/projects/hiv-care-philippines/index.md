description: "The Philippine HIV Care Directory is a fast, accessible, mobile-first web application that helps Filipinos locate DOH-designated HIV treatment hubs and primary care facilities offering Antiretroviral Therapy (ART), Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP), Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP), and HIV testing services across the country.\n\nThe platform exists to reduce friction between people living with or at risk of HIV and the public services already available to them. Information is mirrored faithfully from official DOH issuances and presented in a clean, searchable, shareable interface that works equally well on desktop, tablet, and entry-level mobile devices."
